Fonts in this folder come from Google Fonts (https://github.com/google/fonts) and are
used under their open licences: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (most families) or Apache
License 2.0 (Roboto, Roboto Mono). They were trimmed to Latin characters (plus the
rupee sign) and, for variable fonts, fixed to weights 400 (regular) and 700 (bold).
